## Trending Audio Strategy

1. Check TikTok "Discover" page for trending sounds
2. Use trending audio in first 3 seconds
3. Add your own spin to familiar formats
4. Post when audio is trending (not after peak)

## Engagement Tips

- Reply to ALL comments in first hour
- Use Duet/Stitch features to join trending conversations
- Pin best comment to boost engagement signal
- Go LIVE once/month for Q&A sessions

## Cross-Platform Tips

**TikTok → YouTube Shorts**: Re-upload TikToks to YouTube Shorts (same content, different audience)
**TikTok → Instagram Reels**: Native upload (not Repost) for algorithmic favor
**TikTok → Twitter/X**: Clip best moments as clips

## Monetization Link Strategy

- Link in bio: `tianji.love` (short, memorable)
- Use Linktree or equivalent for multiple links
- Update link to match current campaign (e.g., `/pricing` for sales push)
- Track clicks with UTM parameters:
  ```
  tianji.love?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=week1
  ```
