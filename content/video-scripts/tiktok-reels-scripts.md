## Video Production Notes

### Filming Setup (Budget: $0)
- **Background:** Dark room + fairy lights OR bedroom at night
- **Camera:** iPhone portrait mode, 1080p60fps
- **Lighting:** Ring light (Amazon basics, ~$25) or natural window light
- **Audio:** AirPods or cheap lapel mic

### Editing Tools (Free)
- **CapCut** (mobile) — text overlays, filters, transitions
- **Canva** (desktop) — thumbnail + bio link graphics
- **CapCut Desktop** — auto-captions (CRITICAL for TikTok)
