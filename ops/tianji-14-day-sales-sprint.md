# TianJi 14-Day Sales Sprint

## Goal

Reach the first real paid conversions on `https://tianji.love`.

Revenue is not guaranteed. The operating goal is to make the path measurable, sellable, and repeatable.

## Week 1

### Day 1

Priority:
Configure payment secrets and database.

Tasks:

- Add `STRIPE_SECRET_KEY`.
- Add `STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET`.
- Add `DATABASE_URL`.
- Apply `supabase/migrations/20260501_pay_per_use_purchases.sql`.
- Run one test checkout.

### Day 2

Priority:
Make one page convert.

Tasks:

- Focus all traffic on `/ask`.
- Put examples near the form.
- Add "Preview free, unlock full answer for $1.99."
- Verify mobile flow.

### Day 3

Priority:
Publish proof of experience.

Tasks:

- Record `/ask` screen flow.
- Record `/draw` screen flow.
- Make 5 short clips.
- Make 3 image posts.

### Day 4

Priority:
Open creator pipeline.

Tasks:

- Build list of 50 micro-creators.
- Send first 20 DMs.
- Offer free readings and affiliate split.

### Day 5

Priority:
Test relationship demand.

Tasks:

- Publish 3 relationship-question hooks.
- Send traffic to `/ask`.
- Track clicks and preview starts.

### Day 6

Priority:
Community prompts.

Tasks:

- Post "What question are you circling?"
- Reply with short symbolic interpretations.
- Send interested users to `/ask`.

### Day 7

Priority:
CEO review.

Tasks:

- Count visitors.
- Count preview starts.
- Count checkout attempts.
- Read logs.
- Choose the winning hook.

## Week 2

### Days 8-10

Priority:
Double down on the best hook.

Tasks:

- Publish 3 variants per day.
- Contact 10 creators per day.
- Add creator feedback to copy.

### Days 11-12

Priority:
Improve conversion.

Tasks:

- Tighten above-the-fold copy.
- Reduce friction in form.
- Clarify full unlock value.

### Days 13-14

Priority:
Repeatable channel decision.

Tasks:

- Rank channels by visitor quality.
- Decide next 30-day focus.
- Decide whether to add email capture.
- Decide whether to test subscriptions.

## Stop Doing

- Broad "AI fortune platform" messaging.
- Paid ads before checkout works.
- Building more features before one channel converts.
- Abstract spiritual posts without a click path.
