A: TianJi is a self-reflection tool. It uses symbolic systems to help you name patterns, not to guarantee outcomes.

Q: Do I need an account?
A: No. You can preview first and unlock the full result through checkout.

Q: Is my question private?
A: The experience is designed to be private and lightweight. Do not enter medical, legal, financial, or highly sensitive personal data.

Q: Why pay?
A: The preview shows the direction. The unlock gives the complete reading and reflection prompt.
