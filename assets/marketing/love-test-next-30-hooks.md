# TianJi Love — Next 30 Hooks Pool

Purpose: keep a rolling pool of 30 fresh, grounded hooks for short-form video, carousel, and caption work. Hooks rotate across the four core themes and avoid fake urgency, fake certainty, mind-reading, or guaranteed outcomes.

Pool refreshed: 2026-06-23.
Each hook is reviewed against the safety baseline before publishing. Replace used hooks rather than re-using to keep rotation fresh.

## Safety baseline

- No fake testimonials, fake user counts, fake revenue, fake accuracy.
- No "they will come back", "they still love you", or reunion guarantee.
- No diagnosis, medical, financial, or legal certainty.
- No "send this exact text", "act tonight", or chase framing.
- No private chats, names, birth data, photos, or identifying detail.
- CTA points to `/love-test` first; result pages stay private by default.

## Rotation themes

```
T1 - What is he thinking now?
T2 - Should I take the initiative in ambiguity?
T3 - Will they come back after no contact?
T4 - Is this relationship worth continuing?
T5 - Baseline safety: reflection, privacy, repair, calmer-not-dependent
```
