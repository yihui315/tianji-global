## Channel checks

### TikTok

- Keep video under 30 seconds.
- First two seconds must contain a search-style hook.
- Use captions for silent viewing.
- Use blurred or synthetic UI only.
- Do not show real private messages.

### Instagram Reels

- Reuse the safest TikTok winner.
- Keep profile-link CTA soft.
- Do not claim audience demand or results.

### YouTube Shorts

- Title should match search language.
- Description should mention reflection and privacy.
- No certainty or prediction claim.

### Creator outreach

- Ask for feedback, not endorsement.
- Do not offer fake testimonial copy.
- Do not ask creators to share private relationship stories.
