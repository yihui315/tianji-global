## Theme-specific checks (mind-reading loop)

- Copy frames "what is he thinking" as a *pattern to observe*, not a *mind-reading promise*.
- Copy does not claim TianJi Love Test can reveal the other person's actual thoughts or feelings.
- Copy separates *reading the other person* from *steadying yourself*.
- Copy does not imply the user "deserves" or "does not deserve" to be replied to.
- Hook lines do not shame users for over-interpreting silence.
- `/ask?source=love_test&intent=what_are_they_thinking` is positioned as a next-step preview, not as a guaranteed mind-reading tool.
