## Theme-specific checks (waiting posture)

- Copy frames waiting as a *pattern to observe*, not a verdict on the other person.
- Copy does not nudge the user toward "testing" (e.g., "if you stop replying, you'll see if they care").
- Copy separates *expressing* from *seeking reassurance*.
- Copy does not imply that the user "deserves" or "does not deserve" to be reached out to.
- Hook lines do not shame users for waiting.

### Videohao

### KOL outreach
