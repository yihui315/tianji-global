## Channel-specific checks

### Xiaohongshu

- Use one soft visual or pattern-card style image; no realistic chat screenshots, no draft-message mockups.
- First comment may ask: "写下来之后，你更想先观察，还是先行动？"
- Do not invite users to send messages immediately after seeing a reading.
- Do not offer a copy-paste message script under the Day 11 angle.
- Do not pair Day 11 with "已经憋不住了" or other urgency language.

### TikTok / Reels

- Keep scripts under 30 seconds.
- Hook in the first 3 seconds must be grounding, not a sales pitch and not a "send now" cue.
- Do not pair Day 11 angle with dramatic voiceover, suspense music, or "you should send it today" energy.
- Captions must frame the reading as pause, not as a forecast and not as a script for what to write next.

### X / Twitter

- Keep posts under 280 characters.
- Do not thread a Day 11 post into a "you should message them now" series.
- Do not reply to viral "should I send this message?" drama with the Day 11 copy as a sales reply.
- Do not post a screenshot of a "draft I never sent" mockup.

### Reddit / Quora

- Confirm the host platform's affiliation-disclosure rule before posting.
- Open with the question being answered, not with the TianJi product.
- Place the CTA at the end, in one line, and only once.
- Do not cross-post the same answer to multiple threads in the same day.
- Do not offer a message template or "send after X days" rule in any answer draft.

### KOL DM

- Ask for honest critical feedback, not endorsement.
- Do not request or imply a testimonial.
- Do not offer paid compensation, free product in exchange for review, or any quid-pro-quo.
- Stop the thread if the KOL asks for compensation; the policy is feedback-only.
- Do not ask the KOL to script a "send this message" template.

## Comments moderation plan

- Hide or redirect any comment that asks for a guaranteed outcome ("will they reply if I send this?").
- Hide or redirect any comment that frames a partner with a diagnosis label.
- Hide or redirect any comment that asks for a copy-paste message script or "what should I text them".
- Do not engage in private DMs from comment threads.
- If a comment claims a personal success story, treat it as unverified. Do not amplify.
