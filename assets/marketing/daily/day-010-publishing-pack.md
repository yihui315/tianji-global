## Theme

From the hook pool and the recurring anchor angles (what is he thinking, should I initiate, will they come back, is it worth continuing): "Reading the room vs reading the person — pattern recognition is not mind reading."

Today sits before the Day 7 (06-30) calendar entry, so this pack uses a rotation sub-angle rather than a dated calendar row. The risk of this angle is the user mistaking pattern recognition for prediction: "the pattern says X, so he will do Y." The hook is to hold the line between seeing the rhythm and writing the script. The CTA path is reflective first (`/love-test`, `/relationship/new`, `/love-reading`, or `/ask?source=love_test`), never a forced premium unlock.
