# TianJi Love Day 003 Publishing Pack

Purpose: continue the manual publishing rhythm for `/love-test` traffic. This pack reuses the Phase 1 marketing asset system, stays narrowly focused on one daily theme, and keeps all claims reflective, private, and non-guaranteed.

Publishing date: 2026-06-22

## Daily theme

> 他现在到底在想什么？先别急着追问，先看你们的互动模式。

This day's theme narrows in on the *mind-reading loop*: users who keep replaying the last conversation, who read meaning into reply speed, and who treat every silence as a clue. The pack keeps the first action reflective (`/love-test`), explicitly avoids claiming to know what the other person is thinking, and steers users toward reading a *pattern* rather than chasing a *verdict*.

## Safety baseline

- No fake testimonials.
- No fake user counts.
- No guaranteed relationship outcomes.
- No perfect-accuracy claims.
- No medical or psychological diagnosis.
- CTA points to `/love-test` first.
- Paid-intent copy stays preview/approval-gated; no live payment claim.
- No advice to "test" or provoke the other person.
- No coercion framing ("if they really care, they would…").
- Do not claim to read the other person's actual thoughts or feelings.
