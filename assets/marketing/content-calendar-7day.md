## Theme rotation

This week cycles through the four core TianJi Love angles plus two supporting angles, in this order: *what is he thinking now* → *should I take the initiative in ambiguity* → *will they come back after no contact* → *is this relationship worth continuing* → *how to read push-pull* → *when to reach out* → *what am I really waiting for*. The closing day re-centers on self-steadiness to avoid stacking two "what is he thinking" days back to back.
