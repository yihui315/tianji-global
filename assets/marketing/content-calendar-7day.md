## Daily Publishing Rhythm

```text
Morning: one short-video emotional hook
Noon: one short-video or carousel evidence/pattern angle
Afternoon: one trust or privacy proof point
Evening: one reflective CTA to /love-test or /relationship/new
Late evening: one SEO outline, caption set, or KPI review note
```

## Theme Rotation Notes

```text
Day 004 - anxiety vs evidence
Day 005 - reflection, not mind reading
Day 006 - compatibility as rhythm
Day 007 - the safest next text
Day 008 - emotional entry without dependency
Day 009 - privacy is the product
Day 010 - pattern before optimization
Day 011 - understanding vs acting
Day 012 - repair over replay
```

Rotation avoids repeating one emotional angle (anxiety, urgency, chase) more than twice in any five-day window. Privacy, trust, and "calmer, not more dependent" angles recur by design because they are baseline safety messages, not hype.

## Manual Operator Notes

- Publish only after checking the matching review checklist.
- Fill KPI rows after each post has at least an initial observation window.
- If all metrics are zero, record `published`, `pending`, or `not posted` in notes instead of inventing performance.
- KPI analysis and funnel suggestions are blocked until real non-placeholder KPI rows exist.
- Auto-posting, email sending, checkout execution, paid smoke, production deploy, raw `.env` access, and secret handling remain No-Go.
