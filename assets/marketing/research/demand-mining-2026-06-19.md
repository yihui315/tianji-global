## Audience Pains

1. "They went cold" panic: one short reply or delay becomes a verdict.
2. "Should I text them?" uncertainty: users need a motive check before action.
3. Mixed-signal fatigue: hot/cold behavior creates emotional over-interpretation.
4. Bad-texter confusion: slow replies may be overload, avoidance, preference, anxiety, or disinterest.
5. AI dating skepticism: users may want help thinking, but not a fake chatbot persona.
6. Compatibility curiosity: the market likes tests and scores, but many competitors lean into entertainment, photos, zodiac, or certainty.
7. Privacy concern: relationship users do not want real chats, birth data, photos, or private details exposed in public content.

## Positioning Hypothesis

TianJi Love should not sell "we know what they think." It should sell:

```text

```
