## GEO Page Pattern

Use this structure for each future SEO/GEO asset:

1. H1 states one concrete question.
2. First paragraph gives a direct, safe answer in 2-3 sentences.
3. `What to observe` lists 3-5 pattern signals.
4. `What not to conclude too quickly` blocks certainty and fear escalation.
5. `Private Love Test` CTA points to `/love-test`.
6. FAQ answers privacy, prediction, and paid boundary questions.
7. Disclaimer states reflection support only, not diagnosis, mind-reading, or guarantee.
