# TianJi Love — Next 20 Video Scripts Pool

Purpose: keep a rolling pool of 20 short-form video scripts (15–45 seconds) ready for manual publishing. Scripts cover TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts and stay grounded in the safety baseline.

Pool refreshed: 2026-06-24.
Each script is for human review and manual publishing only. Replace used scripts rather than re-recording identical reads.

## Safety baseline

- No fake testimonials, fake user counts, fake revenue, or fake accuracy claims.
- No guaranteed reunion, prediction, mind-reading, diagnosis, or medical/financial certainty.
- No private chats, screenshots, names, birth data, photos, or identifying detail.
- No "send this exact text", "act tonight", or pressure framing.
- CTA points to `/love-test`. Result pages stay private by default.

## Script template

```text
Hook (1-3s): grounded observation, no promise.
Beat 1 (5-10s): name the pattern, not the other person.
Beat 2 (10-15s): reflection question the viewer can answer themselves.
CTA (3-5s): /love-test is private; you stay in charge of the choice.
Caption: one safety line + one observation prompt.
Risk note: what to cut if it sounds like a guarantee.
```
